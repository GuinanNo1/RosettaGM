MIDI2Move MIDI to Ableton Move converter alpha 0.6

Quickstart · User guide

Turn a General MIDI file into an Ableton Move Set. Pick the parts you want, hear how they'll play, and download an .ablbundle for Move Manager, using the MoveGM sounds.

Alpha 0.6, for testing. Sets open and play in Ableton Live 12; they have not yet been tested on Move hardware. Keep backups of your Move Sets.

1 Load a MIDI file

You're in simple mode: the main choices, with settings that suit most songs. Switch on Advanced for detailed control over how the song is laid out, drum pads, pitch bends and warnings.

You're in Advanced mode: every setting is shown. Switch back to Simple at any time; your settings are kept and still applied.

Sound pack MoveGM v0.2 Loading from this site…

MIDI2Move plays and builds with the MoveGM sounds from this site, so there's nothing to set up. To use another copy instead (your own edited MoveGM, or a newer or older build), choose its folder: it needs MoveGM's layout, "01 Piano" … "18 Drift Synths". Nothing is uploaded. Whichever pack you use, put the same presets on your Move.

2 Choose the parts

Give each part a Move track. Drums is a 16-pad drum kit for the GM drum part (channel 10); T1–T4 are the Move's four tracks (with drums, the drum kit is Track 1). Pads plays a part on spare drum pads, played chromatically: each pad plays one note at a time, so pads suit bass lines and leads, and extend the four tracks. Send several parts to one track to combine them. ▶ plays a single part.

4 Check the arrangement

0:00 The preview plays the converted parts with the MoveGM samples (Drift tracks use their MoveGM sample).

Click a dot to mute or unmute a whole row, or a box to mute or unmute those bars. Mutes change the preview and the Set. The letters are the scene each section plays (a Move Set holds 8 scenes, so long songs need more than one Set); the rows under each track are the MIDI parts feeding it. Click a scene · bar heading to play from there. S solos a track in the preview only.

    5 Build the Set

    Upload the .ablbundle in Move Manager (move.local › Sets). On the Move you launch the scenes by hand, in the play order shown above. You can also open it in Ableton Live 12.4 or later.

    Play it as a song in Ableton Live (optional)

    To hear the whole song play through in Live: 1. open the downloaded Set in Live, 2. choose File › Save Live Set As…, 3. drop that .als file here. You get back a copy where every scene moves on to the next by itself; launch the first scene.
