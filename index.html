RosettaGM General MIDI as a common language for grooveboxes

Like the Rosetta Stone, these tools use one shared language, General MIDI, so that songs and sounds can move between devices that otherwise can't read each other's files. Each device gets a General MIDI sound pack and converters to and from MIDI. Everything runs in your browser: nothing is uploaded.

MoveGM in development

Ableton Move

A General MIDI sound pack for the Ableton Move (128 instruments, 12 drum kits and Drift synths), and MIDI2Move, which turns a MIDI file into a Move Set. Both are being tested and will appear here soon.
