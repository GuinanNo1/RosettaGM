MoveGM General MIDI sound pack for the Ableton Move v0.2

All 128 General MIDI instruments as Move presets, 12 drum kits laid out so General MIDI drum parts play the right pads, and 23 Drift synths as alternatives for the synth sounds. It's the sound set behind MIDI2Move, and you can play it like any other preset pack.

v0.2, for testing. The presets load and play in Ableton Live 12, but haven't been tried on Move hardware yet. Uploading presets doesn't change your Sets, but keep a backup of your Move anyway.

Download and install

Download MoveGM v0.2 Zip, 118 MB. Unzip it to get the MoveGM folder.

  1. On a computer on the same network as your Move, open move.local in a browser and go to the Presets tab.
  2. Drag the whole MoveGM folder onto the page and confirm Upload.
  3. On the Move, browse User Presets › MoveGM. The presets are in 18 folders, one per General MIDI family plus Drum Kits and Drift Synths.

Move keeps all preset samples in one shared folder, so MoveGM's sample files start with MGM_ to keep them apart from your own. MIDI2Move uses the same presets straight from this site, so you don't need the zip for it.

Listen first

Audition every preset: press a pad to hear each one (about 6 MB, plays in the browser). There's also a short demo song as a Move Set, and an offline render of it:

  • MoveGM Demo.ablbundle (instruments and drums; upload in Move Manager › Sets, or open in Live 12)
  • MoveGM Drift Demo.ablbundle (the Drift synths)
  • Demo, offline render (MP3)

Kits 01–09: pads 1–16 play General MIDI drum notes 36–51 (kick, side stick, snare, clap, toms, hi-hats, crash, ride). Move's pads use the same note numbers, so General MIDI drum parts play the right pads unchanged. Kits 10–12 hold the rest of the General MIDI and GS percussion, 16 notes per kit.

Synthesised stand-ins for the General MIDI synth sounds, named "NNN Name (Drift)". Macros: Filter Freq, Filter Reso, Filter Env, Osc 2 Level, Attack, Decay, Release, Glide.

How it was made

  • The Move's Melodic Sampler plays one sample per preset, so each instrument is recorded at one key (C1 to C5, depending on its range) and the preset's transpose puts it back in tune. Notes far from that key sound stretched, most noticeably at the ends of the piano.
  • The Move has no sample loop mode, so looped sounds are unrolled into 8-second samples with a fade.
  • GeneralUser's filter envelopes are baked into the samples at velocity 100, and the Move's filter is left fully open for you to use.
  • Samples are 44.1 kHz, 16-bit WAV. Levels follow GeneralUser's balance through each preset's volume.
  • Known issues: Flute is 11 cents sharp and Rock Organ 12 cents sharp (from GeneralUser's own tuning); these will be fixed in v0.3.

The build scripts and format notes are in the repository.
